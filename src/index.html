Our Story

One Kitchen,
One Cosecha

Casa de Cosecha started in a Tampa kitchen: grass-fed tallow and Florida citrus, nothing shipped in from anywhere else. "Cosecha" means harvest — every bar is a small one, poured by hand, cut by hand, wrapped by hand.

No fillers, no fake lather boosters, no mystery fragrance oil. Just what's ripe, rendered, and ready.

— la familia Cosecha
